Privacy Policy for Sippet

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Sippet is a water tracker with a pet bunny that grows as you reach your goal. It keeps everything on your phone. This policy says exactly what the app stores, what leaves your device (very little), and what you can do about it.

1. What we collect (nothing)

We do not collect, receive, store on a server, or share any personal data. We have no servers, no accounts and no database of users.

2. What the app stores on your phone

Everything below stays in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your drinks (amount, type, time), your daily goal and the goal on each past day.
  • Your profile details used to work out a goal (weight, age, gender, activity level), if you enter them.
  • Your pet: its name, the day it arrived and how far it has grown.
  • Bathroom visits you log in the Health tab (time and urine colour). This is health-related information. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your reminders, wake-up time, bedtime, evening nudge, quick-add amounts, theme and other settings.
  • Whether you have bought Sippet Plus, and a rating and comment if you give one.

The app asks your device for permission to show notifications (reminders, the evening nudge and the progress notification) and to schedule them. Both are optional.

3. Backup to your Google account

On Android, the app lets Android's own backup save your drinks, bathroom visits, your pet and your settings to your Google account, so they come back when you set up a new phone or reinstall the app. This is Google's backup service, not ours: we cannot see or reach it. Google encrypts it, and on phones with a screen lock it is end-to-end encrypted. You can turn it off, or delete it, in your phone's settings (Settings, Google, Backup).

4. Home screen widget

The optional home screen widget shows your pet, today's total and your goal. It reads a small summary and pictures of your pet that the app saves on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

5. What leaves your device

The app does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind. Apart from Android's backup (section 3), the only times anything leaves your phone are things you start:

  • Sippet Plus purchase. Buying Plus, restoring it and checking it go through Google Play and the Google Play Billing Library. Google handles your payment details and tells the app whether you have bought Plus. We never see your card or account details. Google's own privacy policy applies to that part.
  • Rating and comment. After a number of drinks the app may ask you to rate it. If you give four or five stars it opens the store page so you can leave a public review there. If you give fewer stars and write a comment, it opens an email draft addressed to the developer; the comment is only sent if you send that email.
  • Sharing and exporting. If you export your history as CSV, the file is handed to the sharing app you choose. It goes nowhere else.
  • Links. Opening the store page uses your browser or the Play Store.

6. Children

Sippet is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no information from anyone, whatever their age.

7. Your rights (GDPR, POPIA, CCPA and similar)

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your data is on your device, and in your own Google backup if you keep it on, and under your control. "Clear all data" in Settings deletes your drinks, your bathroom visits and your pet's progress. To remove everything, including your profile, reminders and settings, uninstall the app, and delete the app's backup in your phone's settings if you want that gone too.

8. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Its only copy elsewhere is Android's encrypted backup in your own Google account.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app before anything about your data changes.
